Kintsugi

Kintsugi is the Japanese practice of mending broken pottery with seams of gold — the repair made visible instead of hidden. Here, you write through a hard stretch of your life: each difficulty becomes a crack in a rendered bowl, and each thing that carried you through it becomes the gold filling that crack.
